What the numbers point to

1
Set, profile, you

The set's agent.json, the Agent Profile it is measured against, and your name, which goes on the prompts you write.

2
Profile and set disagree

Shown, never fixed silently. Actions come from the profile; targets and states stay in the set.

3
Prompts by category

Ids are made for you, target_012 after target_011. ⚠ marks a prompt that breaks a rule.

4
Dropdowns, not typing

The action list is the profile's plus none; the targets are the set's plus no_target.

5
The README's rules, inline

Registered target, none with no_target, a needed target, the state, ids, and no few-shot example.

6
Label blind

Only the label, stimulus, observations and state. The actions offered are the ones the state allows.

7
One file per person

Two people labelling at once never touch the same file.

8
Agreement

How many answered and agreed, per prompt and overall, and the prompts where they split.

9
Settling stays human

Nothing is written until a person picks the answer, as findings.md says.
