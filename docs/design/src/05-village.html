What the numbers point to

1
The world

Tower, bridge and training dummy are the registered targets: the only things the guard can be sent to. Houses and woods are scenery.

2
Talk radius

Within 5 m of the guard, “Press Enter to talk” appears. Movement pauses while the player types.

3
Say anything

A free text box, not a menu. What the player types is sent as it stands, and the guard's reply comes back on the line above.

4
Thinking

A “…” bubble covers the second or two the model takes, so the game never looks frozen.

5
The action runs

The guard walks to the tower. Our own game code moves it; the model only chose move_to and tower.

6
Decision overlay (F1)

Action, target, latency and which guards ran. This is the view we put on screen at the review demo.
