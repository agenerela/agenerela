What the numbers point to

1
Turn bar

End Turn asks each country for one decision, one after another. The progress squares show who has answered.

2
Map

Four countries and their relations. None of them is a character: no body, no pathfinding, nothing spoken aloud.

3
Country panel

Treasury, army, forts and relations: the game state our own code keeps, exactly as it would without any AI.

4
What it is told

The short sentences sent with the request. The game decides what each country knows, and a country is told only its own facts.

5
Moves offered now

Only legal moves are offered. With an army under 50, Merrow is never even shown declare_war.

6
Event log

Each decision's statement becomes a proclamation, and its action changes the map. Same framework, same fields as the village guard.
