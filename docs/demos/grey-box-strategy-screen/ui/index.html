About this mock-up

A clickable design, not the game. No model runs in this page: the decisions, the replies in advise mode and the rivals' moves are scripted. The rules are real: the moves offered, the reasons a move is not offered, battle strength and upkeep follow GameRules and Legality.

Turn 12 is invented to fit the rules. Lines marked * are quoted from the persona 4b table's seed 4 (11 November); the rest are written for the mock-up. Telemetry values are plausible, not measured.

The map is a generated painting; the 25 provinces are built from the rule board's 5 × 5 graph, so they border exactly as the rules say. Washes, borders and the capture animation are drawn live from that province map, as the Unity shader would.

Keys: Space play/pause · N step · +/− speed · C chronicle · D diplomacy · 1–5 map modes · Esc close. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
